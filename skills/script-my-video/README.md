# Script My Video

An [Agent Skill](https://agentskills.io) that plans a talking-head video before you film it, so the footage can actually be edited into something good.

Most of what sinks an edit happens before the camera rolls: a hook that starts with a wind-up, clips recorded as one-liners that don't flow into each other, a claim nobody can prove on screen, a screen recording nobody captured. This skill fixes those on paper. You get:

- a short interview first, to pull out your real story, numbers and proof
- 2–3 hook variants, word for word, each a different shape, provocative but true
- a sources table for every claim that goes on screen
- a **shoot sheet**: numbered clips, each opening with a bridge line from the last, a question you answer in your own words, talking points, and 1–2 written punchlines; hook and close word for word
- an on-screen plan for every beat, with one visual thread and planned transitions
- a list of screens to capture and B-roll to shoot before filming
- a hook check with [roast-my-short](../roast-my-short/) before you film

Works for Shorts (TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts) and long-form YouTube, including a build that produces both from one shoot.

## Pairs with

- [no-ai-slop-writing](../no-ai-slop-writing/) for your voice in the word-for-word lines
- [make-my-video-awesome](../make-my-video-awesome/) to edit the footage once it's filmed
- [roast-my-short](../roast-my-short/) for the hook check and the review after the edit

## Use it

```text
Plan a Short about how my AI agent reads SEC filings overnight. Interview me first.
```

```text
Turn this idea into a shoot sheet: "I built a retirement planner with AI instead of paying 1% a year."
```

```text
Plan the YouTube video for this build, and pick two Shorts we can cut from the same shoot.
```
