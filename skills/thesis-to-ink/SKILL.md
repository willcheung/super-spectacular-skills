# Thesis to Ink

Use for investment research written for general readers. Start from the latest authorized research packet and the user's direction; preserve their voice without inventing personal opinions, holdings or endorsements.

If `no-ai-slop-writing` is available, use it for the writing and voice pass alongside this research workflow, following its applicable instructions and the user's preferences. If unavailable, write in clear, natural language, preserve the writer's voice and uncertainty, and avoid formulaic filler. Do not install another skill as part of this workflow.

If a completed War Room or other research packet is supplied, consume its source-linked claims, evidence and observation clocks, thesis and countercase, assumptions, contradictions and coverage gaps, proof/falsification gates, and confidence or run-quality status. Verify material claims and preserve unresolved conflicts; a research verdict is not publication or trading authority. Exclude private account and decision content. Use other authorized evidence when no such packet exists; do not automatically run a War Room.

## From evidence to story

1. Identify the economic question and audience. Trace the supported chain from industry trend to customer behavior, product exposure, revenue, margins, cash and valuation. Treat each link as a separate claim: use primary filings or issuer/customer disclosures where available, distinguish fact, management estimate, analyst assumption and scenario, and identify the strongest alternative explanation and falsifiers. A missing link stays unknown. Recheck time-sensitive material claims and reproduce material calculations; retain dated sources, units, scope and lineage to the current packet. Do not create a target or return from unsupported assumptions.
2. Write an original story a curious nonexpert can follow. Start with the concrete tension, explain jargon on first use, then move through opportunity, economic evidence, countercase and observable next checkpoints as the evidence warrants. Use specific question or action headings that help readers navigate, and a single natural closing CTA when useful. Retain uncertainty where it changes the conclusion without repeating the same caveat throughout. Use any user-selected editorial examples for broad techniques, not copied wording or distinctive conceits.
3. Put useful charts where they support the argument. Label each with source, date, units and period; distinguish observations from estimates. Mark causal diagrams as conceptual and expose unsupported links. Choose visuals and length to fit the evidence, not a fixed quota.
4. Keep private decision notes, positions and internal material separate from the public-safe draft. Put detailed source checks, calculations, assumptions, limitations and version lineage in a linked appendix; verify the link works in the delivered format. A draft is unpublished unless a separately authorized action publishes it. This skill grants no publication, subscriber-send, trading or live-system authority.
5. Check the draft against the source packet and acceptance questions below. Qualify or remove unsupported claims and report gaps. Verify rendered layout when the format and environment permit; disclose an unverified visual review.

## Editorial examples

Invented examples of clarity, not facts or stock views:

- Heading: “Working Capital and Liquidity Analysis” → “Who pays before the customer does?”
- Paragraph: “Inventory procurement obligations increased while operating cash conversion was negative.” → “The company has committed to buy more parts while its operations used cash. We still need the payment and collection schedule.”

## Acceptance check

- Can a reader explain what the company sells, why the trend might matter and the economic question without specialist vocabulary?
- Do headings carry the argument, with a fair countercase and concrete next observations?
- Is every material number and causal claim traceable to a dated source or explicitly labeled calculation/assumption? Are periods, units, cash definitions and uncertainty accurate?
- Do charts support the adjacent argument, and are conceptual or estimated links labeled? Does the appendix link resolve in the delivered artifact?
- Are private decision material and unsupported targets absent from the public-safe draft? Are current version, source lineage and publication status clear?
