# Thesis to Ink

Turn an investment research packet into a readable newsletter without losing the evidence. The skill follows the path from industry trend to customer, product, company economics, cash and valuation; it keeps counterarguments and unknowns visible and puts detailed calculations in a linked appendix.

Use `$thesis-to-ink` with a research packet, source links or an existing draft. If `no-ai-slop-writing` is installed, the skill uses it for the voice and writing pass. It remains usable without that optional skill. A supplied War Room or similar research packet can provide source-linked claims and open questions; the skill does not require or launch one.

The output is an unpublished draft unless publication is separately authorized. The skill does not grant trading or distribution authority. See the [repository installation instructions](../../README.md#install).
