Growth and inflation assumptions

Returns are nominal totals after fees and recurring investment-tax drag, before separate withdrawal taxes. The late path reverses the full expanded return sequence. Deterministic scenarios do not supply success probabilities.

How much investment growth does the plan need?

These estimates use steady yearly growth and the same retirement date. Ending with your original buying power still allows your balance to fall along the way.

Annual cash needs and college lump sums

Living withdrawals are monthly equivalents, excluding college. College and event draws are separate annual amounts. Actual draws may be below required draws after depletion. The annual model deducts the full year's cash needs before growth.
